# Cinnamon Sugar Chocolate Chip Pumpkin Bread

Cinnamon Sugar Chocolate Chip Pumpkin Bread:

Ingredients:
½ cup pumpkin puree (*overflowing*)
½ cup avocado oil (melted coconut oil or butter works too!)
½ cup maple syrup
2 eggs
2 tsp vanilla extract
1 ½ cup all-purpose flour
2 tsp baking powder
½ tsp baking soda
½ tsp cinnamon½ tsp nutmeg
¼ tsp ground ginger
½ cup chocolate chips
Cinnamon sugar: 1/3 cup of sugar + 1 tsp cinnamon

Directions:
1.     Preheat oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit
2.     Combine all wet ingredients and mix well
3.     Add in dry ingredients and chocolate chips, mix until combined- don’t overmix!
4.     Pour half of the batter into greased loaf pan, then sprinkle half of cinnamon sugar mixture on top
5.     Pour the rest of the batter into the loaf pan and the remaining cinnamon sugar mixture on top.
6.     Take a butter knife and press into batter and swirl around.
7.     Bake for 40-50 minutes
