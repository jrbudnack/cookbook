5 lb pork butt @ 275-300F
- 2026-06-30 - In @ 11:15am, Out 7:15pm = 8 hour cook

Smoke fat-side down
## Ingredients
- 5 pound bone-in pork shoulder (AKA Boston butt) AKA Boston butt
- 2-3 Tablespoons [yellow mustard](https://www.amazon.com/Frenchs-Classic-Yellow-Mustard-Gluten/dp/B00CHTUJQC?&linkCode=ll1&tag=hegrehe-at-cse-rc-20&linkId=b8ff992aff40b1fe0547d885e965b15a&language=en_US&ref_=as_li_ss_tl)
- Make a batch of [Sweet Rub](./Sweet Rub.md)
- After rubbing on pork, keep back 1 Tablespoon [Sweet Rub](./Sweet Rub.md) for later
## Instructions
- **Preheat the smoker.** Preheat your smoker to 225 degrees F for indirect smoking. Use 1/2 hickory and 1/2 apple or peach for this recipe
- **Prep the meat.** Remove your roast from the packaging and wipe it down on all sides with paper towels, cleaning off any small bone fragments or extra liquid on the exterior.
- **Slather the pork butt in mustard.** Slather the entire exterior of the pork shoulder with the yellow mustard.
- **Season with Sweet Rub.** Season your pork roast on all sides, top and bottom, with the Homemade BBQ Sweet Rub. Don't worry about rubbing the seasoning into the meat, just be sure it is liberally coated all over.
- **Add Thermometers**.  Put thermometer probes into the roast, 1 in front, 1 in back
- **Smoke the pork butt.** Place your seasoned roast on the smoker FAT SIDE DOWN, preferably in the middle of the grate avoiding any direct hot spots. Close the door and smoke the pork until it reaches an internal temperature of at least 195 degrees F. You can cook to 201 degrees F if you like softer pork.
- **Wrap and rest the meat.** Remove the pork shoulder from the smoker and wrap tightly in foil. Put the roast into a cooler.  Allow the roast to rest for at least an hour before shredding.
- **Shred and serve.** Pull apart the shoulder, discarding any chunks of fat or gristle. Sprinkle the roast with an additional tablespoon or so of the Homemade Sweet BBQ Rub. Serve and enjoy!
