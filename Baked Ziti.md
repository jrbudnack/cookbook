# Baked Ziti

INGREDIENTS:

-1 pound hot Italian sausage
 
-1 pound sweet Italian sausage

-2 tablespoons olive oil

-1 large yellow onion, diced

-½ teaspoon dried oregano

-¼ teaspoon dried thyme

-¼ teaspoon dried rosemary

-2 (24-ounce) jars prepared marinara sauce

-2 cups water

-1 (16-ounce) package ziti pasta

-1 drizzle olive oil, or as needed, for greasing

-1 ½ cups whole-milk ricotta cheese

-8 ounces mozzarella cheese, cut into small cubes

-1 cup grated Pecorino Romano cheese

-1 teaspoon chopped flat-leaf parsley


DIRECTIONS:

STEP 1:
Gather the ingredients. Cut down the length of each sausage with a sharp knife. Remove and discard casings.

STEP 2:
Heat olive oil in a large saucepan set over high heat. Add sausages and onion. Cook and stir until sausage is crumbly and browned and onion turns translucent, 5 to 7 minutes. Add oregano, thyme, and rosemary; cook for 1 minute more.

STEP 3:
Pour in marinara sauce. Pour 1 cup water into each jar to rinse out remaining sauce; add to the saucepan. Stir well and bring sauce to a simmer.

STEP 4:
Reduce heat to medium-low and let simmer, stirring occasionally, for 1 hour. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed. Remove from heat. Before using sauce, skim any excess fat that rises to the surface.

STEP 5:
While sauce simmers, bring a large pot of generously salted water to a boil. Add ziti and cook, stirring occasionally, for 1 or 2 minutes less than the package directions call for, about 8 minutes. Drain well and transfer to a large bowl.

STEP 6:
Carefully add meat sauce to ziti and stir until thoroughly combined. Let rest for 5 minutes. Meanwhile, preheat the oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C). Grease a large 9x15-inch casserole dish with a drizzle of olive oil. If you need to use a 9x13-inch dish, reserve about 1 cup cooked pasta to avoid overfilling and bubbling over.

STEP 7:
Use a slotted spoon to transfer 1/2 of the pasta-and-sauce mixture to the prepared dish, then use a spatula to spread evenly. Top with 1/2 of the ricotta cheese, 1/2 of the mozzarella cheese, and then 1/2 of the grated Pecorino Romano cheese, being sure to distribute evenly. Top with the remaining pasta-and-sauce mixture and shake the pan gently to settle the sauce. Repeat the layering process with remaining cheese.

STEP 8:
Bake in the center of the preheated oven until cheese is melted and the casserole is piping hot, 30 to 35 minutes. Remove from the oven and let cool for 10 to 15 minutes before serving. Top with parsley.
