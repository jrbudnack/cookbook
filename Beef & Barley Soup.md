# Beef & Barley Soup

2 tbsp butter
1 medium onion diced
1 pound ground beef
1.5 tsp salt
1 tsp black pepper
1 tsp onion powder
1 tsp garlic powder
1 tsp paprika
1 tsp oregano
2 quarts broth
2 cans tomato sauce 15 oz each
2 cups frozen mixed vegetables
1 cup pearl barley

Instructions: 

Stovetop Method
In your pot or dutch oven, melt your butter and sauté your onion until translucent (about 5 minutes).
Add your ground beef and cook until browned. Rinse fat if desired and add seasonings.
To your pot add broth, tomato sauce, vegetables and barley. Cover and simmer on medium low heat for 1 hour.
Serve your warm soup alongside cornbread, rolls or biscuits and enjoy!

Slow Cooker Method
Sauté onion and brown ground beef per instructions above.
Add cooked onion and seasoned beef, broth, tomato sauce, vegetables and barley to slow cooker insert.
Cook on low for 6-8 hours or high for 2-3 hours
