## Ingredients
* 4 Turkey Legs (2 - 2-packs)
* 2 Turkey Thighs (1 - 2-pack)
* Celery
* 1 white onion
* 1 Red Pepper
* 1 smaller red onion
* Hot Banana Peppers
* 1 Package Pita Bread
* 1/2 tsp garlic powder
* Salt and Pepper
## Instructions
* Line bottom of pot with celery stalks.  Rinse off parts  Put 1 cup of water into the pot.  Add turkey parts on top of celery, plus salt and pepper.  Put crock pot on high for 1 hour, low for another 6-7 hours.
- Additional spices and prep
	- 1 white onion
	- 1 head of garlic
- Make Garlic Homefries
- Slice up red peppers and red onion.  Saute them with some hot banana peppers 
	- Heat some olive oil in the pan on medium-high heat, add peppers and onions plus 1/2 tsp garlic powder
	- After 5 minutes, add some salt and pepper
- Heat up some Pita bread
