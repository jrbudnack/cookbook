## Ingredients
- 1 Bag of Sauerkraut
- 2 tbsp butter
- Pinch of ground black pepper
- 3oz "kids pack" of applesauce
- 1 teaspoon caraway seeds

## Instructions
- Drain and squeeze all the liquid out of the sauerkraut
- Heat frying pan on medium heat
- Melt the butter in the pan
- Add sauerkraut and season with pepper
- Add the applesauce and caraway seeds
- Heat for 2-3 minutes, stirring occasionally until warmed throughout
