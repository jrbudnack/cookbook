# Cajun Sausage & Rice Casserole

- 12-14 oz smoked sausage or andouille sausage, sliced into ¼-inch rounds
- 1½ cups uncooked long-grain white rice
- 1 can (10 oz) Rotel diced tomatoes & green chilies, undrained
- 2½ cups chicken broth
- 1 small green bell pepper, diced
- 1 small red bell pepper, diced
- ½ medium yellow onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1½ tsp Cajun seasoning, plus more to taste
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tbsp butter, cut into small pieces
- 2 green onions, sliced, for serving

Instructions:
Preheat oven to 375°F and lightly grease a 9×13-inch baking dish.
Add the uncooked rice, Rotel with its juices, chicken broth, bell peppers, onion, garlic, Cajun seasoning, smoked paprika, thyme and Worcestershire directly to the baking dish. Stir everything together.
Add the sliced smoked sausage and stir it throughout the rice mixture.
Dot the top with the butter.
Cover tightly with foil and bake for 45 minutes.
Remove the casserole and stir it thoroughly, especially around the edges. Check the rice. If it's still a little firm, cover again and bake for another 10–15 minutes, or until the rice is tender and has absorbed most of the liquid.
Let it stand for 5–10 minutes, then fluff the rice and sprinkle with green onions.
